Independent scan recovery uses RapidOCR (RapidAI, Apache-2.0) through rapidocr-onnxruntime 1.4.4 and its packaged PP-OCRv4 models (PaddleOCR contributors, Apache-2.0). Model SHA-256 values are checked in scan_evidence.py. The reader runs locally on CPU; no document upload or runtime model download is used.

Models and fonts are not included in this source repository. Their upstream licenses and attributions apply separately. Dependency and asset notices must accompany a future binary distribution. This is a source-based development installation, not a bundled standalone EXE release.

The 3D word-point view uses Qt Quick 3D through
PySide6-Addons 6.10.3. Qt documents Qt Quick 3D as available under commercial
or GPLv3 terms: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtquick3d-index.html
Qt and PySide6 runtime files are installed as dependencies and are not copied
into this source repository. Their accompanying license notices apply.
Local word graph layout uses NetworkX (BSD-3-Clause):
https://github.com/networkx/networkx/blob/main/LICENSE.txt

The optional installer downloads llama.cpp b11718 (MIT) and its Windows CUDA
12.4 runtime binaries (NVIDIA terms) from the official release:
https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b11718

https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-MT2-30B-A3B-GGUF
